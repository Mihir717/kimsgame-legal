Privacy Policy for Kim's Game

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Kim's Game ("the app") is published by Aegisreplay ("we"). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Your account

You sign in with your Google account. We receive your Google account ID, email address and name from Google, so we can save your game to your account. We never post anything for you and never see your Google password.

What we save

Your game progress (levels, scores, stars, streaks, settings and memory results) is stored on your phone and backed up to our servers, which are run by Google Firebase (Google LLC). It is linked to your account so it follows you to a new phone. Only you can read your saved game.

Leaderboards

If you play the Memory Gym, your nickname (which you choose, not your real name) and your best scores are shown publicly on global leaderboards to other players. Your Google name and email are never shown. You can report a nickname you find offensive. Deleting your account removes your leaderboard entries.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example which levels people play and where they stop) and Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes. These collect an app user ID, device and app-version details, approximate location at country level (from your network address, not your GPS), and what you do in the app. In the EU and UK this starts only after you agree in the consent pop-up. You can change your choice in Settings → "Ad & privacy choices".

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob after each attempt. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and general usage data to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK ads are shown only according to your consent choice. Detective Club members don't see ads. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm what you bought and unlock the content. RevenueCat receives your app user ID and purchase details (what you bought and when), but not your card details. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your phone only.

Location

The app never asks for your location. Country-level information described above comes from your network address.

Children

The app is for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → "Delete my account and data". This permanently deletes your account and your saved game from our servers. You can also email us at kimsgamesupport@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days. Analytics data that is already anonymised may be kept. Subscriptions are cancelled in Google Play (Settings → "Manage or cancel subscription").

Sharing

We share information only with the services above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play and RevenueCat) to run the app. We don't sell your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above.
